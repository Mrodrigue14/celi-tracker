TFSA Tracker (CELI Tracker in French) · Effective October 14, 2026

## In short

The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything
you enter stays on your device. The developer receives none of your data.

## What the app stores

On your device only:

- your year of birth and, if you enter it, your FHSA opening date;
- the annual TFSA limits;
- the deposits and withdrawals you record (account, date, type, amount);
- the CRA figures you enter (account, date, amount);
- the address of the CRA page used to read the new limit, and the date it was
  last read;
- your theme choice.

## Network access

The app connects to the internet for one thing only: reading the canada.ca page
that announces the year's TFSA limit. It does this at most once a month, and
only when a limit is missing, or when you ask for it in the settings. The
request is a plain HTTPS page load. It sends none of your data. The
Government of Canada's server sees your IP address, as it would for any visit
to its website.

## Backups and exports

If backup is turned on for your device, Android copies the app's data to your
Google account and restores it on a new phone. Google keeps this backup
encrypted under your Google account, and the developer has no access to it. You
can manage or delete it in your Google account settings.

The export button writes a JSON file wherever you choose. The app doesn't send
it anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings. To delete the
Android backup too, remove it from your Google account.

## Children

The app is meant for adults: TFSA room starts at 18.
